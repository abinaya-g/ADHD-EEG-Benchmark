## Notes and recommended follow-ups before submission

1. **Row 10 (CNN+LR and other classifier confidence intervals) — RESOLVED during this audit.** An earlier draft of the manuscript's Table 4 contained approximated, not file-extracted, CI bounds for CNN+LR, CNN+LR_withCORAL, CNN+NLSVM, CNN+KNN, CNN+RF, CNN+LinearSVM, and CNN+GNB. All seven were re-extracted directly from `TABLE_4_CONFIDENCE_INTERVALS.csv` during this audit pass and the manuscript's Table 4 was corrected in place to match exactly (the most significant correction was CNN+LR's AUC interval, originally written as 0.729–0.860 versus the file's actual 0.713–0.880). This is flagged here rather than silently fixed, per this project's standing practice of recording what was wrong and how it was caught, not just the final corrected number.
2. **Rows 21–23 (external literature numbers)**: these are not results of this project's own experiments; they are figures reported by other authors, found via web search during literature review, and used only for contextual comparison in Sections 2.3 and 5.8. They should be treated with the same caution the manuscript itself expresses — we have not read the full text of [11] or [12] and cannot independently confirm their exact methodology beyond what the search summaries reported.
3. **Row 24 (§4.7 CORAL CI staleness) — RESOLVED during this audit.** This was a regression left over from item 1's fix: Table 4 was corrected, but the CORAL discussion paragraph in §4.7, which restates the same two CI bounds in prose, was not updated at the same time and still read 0.659–0.807 / 0.667–0.816. Caught while locating where to insert the CORAL figure (Figure 6) into the manuscript, by re-reading §4.7 against the already-corrected Table 4 directly above it. Corrected in place; no other section of the manuscript was found to restate a Table 4 interval in prose without matching it.
4. No numerical value in the manuscript was found to be copied from the earlier, superseded partial runs (the Sep 21 snapshot with DeepConvNet at 1/5 repeats, or the resolution-only run with an incomplete Table 6) — every value traces to the final, 34,544-row combined result set.
5. No numerical value in the manuscript was found to be copied or adapted from any earlier draft's reported numbers; all Results-section figures are original computations from this project's own re-run pipeline.
6. **Row 20/25 (Table 5) — RESOLVED during this audit, the most substantial correction in this project's history.** The model-comparison procedure behind Table 5 was re-derived from first principles because the raw prediction-level file (`results_final/predictions/all_predictions.csv`) had not previously been available for this kind of check; it was located, verified to exactly reproduce Table 2 and Table 3 (to 1e-5), and then used to build a proper subject-level (n=121) paired test, replacing a per-fold (n=25) Wilcoxon test that reused the same 121 subjects across repetitions and was pseudo-replicated as a result. Three comparisons (CNN+KNN, CNN+NLSVM, CNN+RF) lost statistical significance under the corrected test. Full detail in `STATISTICAL_AUDIT.md`.
7. **Table 5 refined a second time — RESOLVED during a follow-up audit.** The permutation test was made the primary p-value (bootstrap reported as an uncertainty estimate, not a second p-value); CNN+LR_noCORAL was removed from the Holm family as a numerically-identical duplicate of CNN+LR (family size 11 → 10); and a direct CNN+LR-vs-CNN+LR_withCORAL test was added (permutation p = 1.0, bootstrap p = 0.775 — statistically confirmed absence of a difference). No comparison's significance conclusion changed; see `STATISTICAL_AUDIT.md` Section 8.
8. **Figure/table model-count reconciliation — RESOLVED during the same follow-up audit.** Figures 3–6 show 10 of the 12 raw model labels (CNN+LR_noCORAL merged into CNN+LR; CNN+LR_withCORAL shown separately in Figure 7), while Table 3/4 show 11 rows (only the CNN+LR/CNN+LR_noCORAL merge) and Table 4 has 20 model×preprocessing groups (8 models at both conditions, 4 at 128 Hz only). This was previously unstated; a reconciliation paragraph was added to Section 4.1 and each affected figure caption now states explicitly which models it does and does not include.
9. **Nine references completed — RESOLVED during the same follow-up audit.** `polanczyk2007worldwide`, `lohani2025systematic`, `hassan2024convolutional`, `esas2023detection`, `sanchis2026multiscale`, `zhong2023deep`, `roy2019deep`, `latifi2024siamese`, and `cura2024detection` were missing volume and/or page/article-number fields; all nine were independently re-verified via web search and completed in `references.bib` (DOIs added where confirmed; `cura2024detection`'s DOI left unset, as it could not be independently confirmed — not fabricated). This search also caught and corrected two author-name errors already present in the bibliography (`lohani2025systematic`'s second author is Vaishali Chawla, not "Vaibhav"; `zhong2023deep`'s full author list was corrected against the paper's own byline).
