## Diagnostic analysis of the Gate 5 / Gate 6 failures (manual; written 2026-09-29)

**Status: STOP.**
- Gates 5 and 6 failed (hard-stop condition: "S1 cannot recover HI; S2 cannot recover HB").
- Nothing was patched. No parameter or criterion was changed.

### 1. Is it an implementation defect? No evidence of one.
- **Likelihood nesting.** The models are nested, so on the same data ll(HB) ≥ ll(HI) and
  ll(HR) ≥ ll(HB) must hold.
  - Across 78,147 logged epochs, this held except in 2 cases.
  - Those cases are at eigenvalue-floor level; max ll(HI) − ll(HB) = 0.
- **Unit tests.**
  - T-BIC: the log-likelihood matches `scipy.stats` exactly.
  - Partition recovery on block-Gaussian data passes.
  - T-BIC on i.i.d. Gaussian data selects the true model:
    - HI and HB at rate 1.0;
    - HR at rate 1.0 for D = 20 and 0.3 for D = 10.
- **Other gates.**
  - Gate 7 (S3 → HR) passes with a very large margin.
  - The FE, leakage and shadow gates pass.

Further observations:
- The adjusted Rand index of the estimated S2 partition against the true blocks is ≈ 0 in
  every run.
- The single-source selectors disagree on S1 D10: HBA-only picks HI in 0.31 of epochs,
  MPA-only in 0.65. The DECOUPLED placebo (column-permuted MPA) moves toward HI/HB. This
  confirms that the detected dependence is cross-coordinate structure *in the displacement
  sample*.

### 3. Interpretation
This is the materialisation of risks **R-BIC-4** (operator geometry rather than landscape),
**R-BIC-3** (non-stationarity) and **R-BIC-5** (elite-directed moves).
1. The "successful native displacements" contain **strong, transient cross-coordinate
   correlation**.
   - HBA and MPA move every agent toward a common best point, so displacements roughly
     reproduce the current population geometry.
   - That geometry is shared by all rows of an iteration.
2. **The samples are far from independent.**
   - A window of W = 60–100 rows comes from only about 6–16 iterations of the same 30
     agents.
   - The in-window BIC treats them as n independent observations, so its evidence for
     richer structure is strongly overstated.
3. **The detected structure does not generalise.**
   - On S1 and S2, a model fitted on OLD predicts CUR best with HI in ≈ 92% of D = 20
     epochs, while the in-window BIC chooses HR in ≈ 90%.
   - The structure is therefore largely transient. It is not the landscape's structure.
4. **The S3 success cannot be separated from this artefact.**
   - The same selectors choose HR on S1, S2 and S3 alike.
   - Gate 7's pass therefore does *not* show that the landscape rotation is detected.

### 4. Consequence for the research question
- As specified, Stage 2's structural evidence does **not** identify landscape structure on
  the primary benchmarks. S1 D20 and S2 at both dimensions fail.
- This removes the basis for the E4 comparisons:
  - JOINT vs POOLED accuracy on S1–S3 would compare selectors that are mostly measuring the
    same artefact.
  - JOINT and POOLED both choose HR in about 90% of epochs on S1 D20 and S2.
- **This is a negative mechanism result, and it is reported as such.**

### 5. Options (each requires the user's decision; none has been applied)
- **(a) Record the negative result and stop N1 as specified.** The premise that successful
  native displacements of HBA/MPA reveal landscape structure fails in this design.
- **(b) A new specification revision** that addresses the diagnosed causes. Examples:
  - effective-sample-size or dependence-aware evidence (e.g. one row per agent per
    epoch, or block-bootstrap BIC);
  - using the prequential PLL as the selection criterion;
  - whitening the displacements by the current population geometry.
  - This would be a **method change after seeing smoke results**.
    - It would need its own pre-registration and a fresh smoke run on new seeds.
    - It must be reported as a post-smoke redesign.
    - The PLL numbers above show that PLL alone would fail S3 at D = 20 (HI 0.79), so no
      option is known to pass all gates.
- **(c) Re-scope** to an analysis of the displacement-structure artefact itself, as a
  characterisation of operator geometry. This is a different research question.

Stage 4 is **not** permitted.
