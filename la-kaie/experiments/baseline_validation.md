# Baseline validation (HBA, MPA, MPHB, MPHBS)

Detailed per-instance numbers: `experiments/baseline_validation_results.md`
and `results/validation/baseline_validation.csv` (generated by
`scripts/compare_baseline_validation.py`).

## What was reproduced and how

| method | source ported | configuration |
|---|---|---|
| HBA | authors' `HBA.m` (Hashim et al. 2021 code) | β = 6, C = 2 |
| MPA | authors' `MPA.m` (Faramarzi et al. 2020 code) | P = 0.5, FADs = 0.2 |
| MPHB | authors' `MPHB_baseline.m` | as above |
| MPHBS | authors' `MPHBS_main.m` | CEC2022: B-C1 (K=7, N_i=3, ρ=0.25, w_best=0.05, γ=0.3, FAD before); FIR: A-C1 (K=3, N_i=9, same, FAD after) |

Every missing-from-paper detail and every paper-vs-code discrepancy is listed
in `reference/MPHBS_NOTES.md`; the port follows the authors' code (which
produced the paper's tables). Nothing was changed to favour LA-KAIE.

## Checks

1. **FE budgets (exact).** Iteration counts are calibrated with the authors'
   FE-probe procedure. Our totals equal the authors' audit file and the
   paper's Table 2 exactly: CEC2022 HBA/MPA 199,980, MPHB 199,950, MPHBS
   199,930; FIR HBA/MPA/MPHB 150,000, MPHBS 149,857
   (`tests/test_baselines.py::test_fe_budgets_match_authors_audit`).
2. **Per-iteration FE accounting and bounds** for every baseline
   (unit tests), best value reported by each algorithm equal to the best value
   actually evaluated (independent tracker; also checked for every run by
   `scripts/validate_experiment.py`).
3. **Distributional agreement with the authors' 30 published MATLAB runs**
   (our campaign `baseline_validation`: 10 runs per instance, seeds disjoint
   from the main experiment; two-sided Mann–Whitney U, Holm within domain):

| domain | comparisons | significant after Holm | smallest raw p | Kendall τ of per-instance method rankings | median ratio ours/authors (median, range) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CEC2022 | 48 | 0 | 0.013 (HBA, F3) | 0.889 | 1.000 (0.336 – 9.8) |
| FIR | 32 | 0 | – | 0.875 | 1.049 (0.529 – 1.35) |

4. **Global pattern.** With MPHBS as anchor on our CEC2022 validation runs the
   instance-level W/T/L versus HBA / MPA / MPHB is 12/0/0, 9/0/3, 6/0/6; the
   paper's Table 13 reports 12/0/0, 8/0/4, 6/0/6.

## Caveats (reported, not hidden)

* MATLAB random streams cannot be reproduced in NumPy; reproduction is
  distributional, not bitwise. Non-significance with 10 vs 30 runs is weak
  evidence of equivalence; the median ratios and rank agreement are the more
  informative indicators.
* **MPHBS on CEC2022 F10**: our median error is 100.4 versus the authors' 10.2
  (ratio 9.8; not significant, p_Holm = 1). F10 is bimodal (runs end near 0
  or near 100; the authors report mean 51.5, std 58.9), so a 10-run median is
  unstable. This instance is re-examined on the 30-run main data before any
  conclusion is drawn.
* HBA on F1/F3 shows larger ratios (4.2, 7.4) but also non-significant
  differences; HBA is highly variable on these functions in both
  implementations.

## Verdict

The baseline ports reproduce the published budgets exactly and the published
result distributions within the resolution of the available evidence. No
STOP condition (inability to reproduce the benchmark or MPHBS) was triggered.
